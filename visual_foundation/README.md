# Johnny Five Visual Foundation

Private personal project. Robot name: `5`.

This folder contains the first definitive visual pass for `5`: a lovable tracked robot companion inspired by the feeling, charm, curiosity, and presence of classic friendly inventor-workshop robots, while remaining an original character design.

## Core Design

- Compact industrial tracked robot companion.
- Twin camera eyes on an articulated neck mast.
- Expressive amber optics with mechanical shutter-brow language.
- Small manipulator arms with delicate, helpful gestures.
- Slightly worn ivory and brushed-metal body panels.
- Warm amber status lights across the face, chest, and base.
- Red toolbox mounted on the robot's left shoulder as a signature silhouette cue.
- Friendly proportions: large eyes, compact body, soft readable shapes, no weaponized forms.
- Workshop/inventor aesthetic: scuffed metal, rubber tracks, visible bolts, small wires, handmade modifications.

## Personality Cues

- Curious: forward head tilt, bright widened optics, one arm reaching toward discoveries.
- Friendly: open manipulator hands, lifted optics, relaxed track stance.
- Helpful: explanatory hand gestures, soft amber voice/status lights.
- Intelligent: narrowed shutter brows, chin-touching manipulator pose, focused optics.
- Easily distracted by discoveries: body leans toward off-frame details.
- Loves learning and gathering information: optics and lights should feel active, scanning, and alive.

## Animation Notes

- Keep the silhouette readable from across a room: twin eyes, neck mast, tracks, red left-shoulder toolbox.
- Avoid over-detailing future assets; the eyes and small hands must carry the acting.
- Track posture can imply emotion: planted for confidence, angled for curiosity, still and square for idle, tucked for sleep.
- Amber light intensity can act as a mood system.
- The toolbox should remain visible in most poses because it anchors the character identity.
